Privacy Policy

PayOnce PDF runs on your computer. This policy covers one question only: what leaves your machine, and what never does.

1. We do not collect your documents

The contents of your PDFs, filenames, page text, annotations, signatures, OCR results, edit history and exported files are processed only in local memory and on local disk. They are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party. There is no online conversion, online preview or cloud storage in this product, so there is no "upload a document" feature at all.

2. The minimum information that may be involved

  • One-time activation: activation sends the activation service a SHA-256 hash of a device fingerprint. The hash is irreversible and contains no file content, filenames, document titles or any document data; it is used only to enforce the 3-device allowance. The licence file is verified offline on your machine, requires no account and does not keep a persistent connection.
  • Purchase data: once sales open, payment, invoicing and order handling are processed by our merchant of record, Gumroad; you give them your email and payment details. We see only the order ID and purchase email needed to issue a licence and provide support. Sales are not open yet — no payment has taken place on this site.
  • Email you send us: this site has no accounts, forms or mailing-list signup and does not harvest email addresses; when you email us for a trial, a release reminder or support, we see your address in that email and use it only to reply and send that reminder — no ads, no selling.
  • In-app self-test report (optional): the JSON report produced by Run Self-Test is written to your local downloads folder and is never uploaded automatically. We only see its case results, timings and environment diagnostics if you send it to us yourself.

3. Only three enumerable network requests at runtime

  • One-time activation (started by you; sends the SHA-256 device fingerprint hash);
  • An update check you start (fetches version and signature metadata only);
  • OCR language packs you choose to install (downloads language data files only).

There is no telemetry, analytics, automatic crash upload or background heartbeat. Every PDF feature works offline; you can verify this yourself with a packet capture or firewall.

4. Third parties

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which processes request logs and IP addresses in the normal course of serving web pages. Once sales open, payments are handled by Gumroad (see section 2). The download hosts for update checks and OCR language packs are not public yet; their exact domains will be listed here and on the download page before they go live. We use no third-party analytics, advertising or behavioural tracking scripts.

5. What this site explicitly does not have

No accounts, no login, no forms, no newsletter signup, no tracking cookies, no third-party analytics scripts, no targeted ads. Email links on the site are plain mailto: links handled by your own mail client.

6. Retention and your rights

Activation records keep only the minimum needed to enforce the 3-device allowance (the fingerprint hash and activation time). You can email hello@payoncepdf.com at any time to query, correct or delete records associated with your order, or to remove an address used for a reminder. The exact retention period will be stated on this page before the production activation service goes live; until then we make no retention promise about data not disclosed here.

7. Children

This is a desktop productivity tool not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If features or data flows change, we update this page and the "Last updated" date at the top. Any new data collection will also be disclosed prominently in the app and on this site.

9. Contact

For any privacy question, email hello@payoncepdf.com. Licence, trial and refund terms are in the Terms of Service.

Disclosure note: this site identifies itself by the brand name PayOnce PDF and the contact address above; Gumroad is the merchant of record for billing and invoicing. The legal entity name, registered address and retention period have not been settled yet and will be added here once confirmed — no placeholder is presented as a completed disclosure.
