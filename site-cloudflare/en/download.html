Or verify from the command line

Windows: certutil -hashfile PayOnce-PDF-Setup-1.0.0.exe SHA256; macOS / Linux: shasum -a 256 <file>.

Installation and safety

Whitelisted open-source components; no AGPL-licensed code.

Is the installer safe?

We keep a whitelist of open-source components (PDFium, qpdf, Tesseract, lopdf) and avoid AGPL-licensed code. Updates are signature-verified, with the previous version kept if verification fails. The current installer is not code-signed yet, so verify its SHA-256 before running it; the checksum is published on this page.

Do I need an account or a card for the trial?

No. The 7-day full trial needs no account and no credit card, and there's no automatic charge at expiry. We won't turn you into a subscriber without your say-so.

What happens when the trial ends? Are my files deleted?

No file is deleted or modified — only export is paused. Users who haven't bought see an in-app payment window: click to pay on Gumroad, then enter the activation key. Buyers who already activated switch to the lifetime buyout automatically and see no window.

Is the trial feature-complete?

Yes. All 16 tools are unlocked for 7 days with no feature gating, daily caps or file-size limits — only continued use after expiry requires a buyout license.
