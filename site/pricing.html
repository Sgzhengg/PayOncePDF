Upgrades & refunds

2.x upgrades and the 7-day refund.

12 months of 1.x updates

Every 1.x release within 12 months of purchase is included. The licence itself never expires — after that window you can keep using your version indefinitely; it does not expire or disable itself. Only the free-update window is 12 months; lifetime updates are not included.

2.x at 50% off for existing users

A future 2.x major version is a separate purchase. Existing 1.x customers get it at half price as a loyalty discount. This is a discount, not a free upgrade.

7-day no-questions refund

Request a refund within 7 days of purchase through the storefront or by email. No reason needed, no account to cancel.

7-day trial, no email

Try every feature for 7 days without signing up. When the trial ends, export is limited but your files are never deleted (error E2005).

Teams

Team purchasing, without accounts.

Small teams are an explicit target (PRD §2.2): one purchase, no per-seat login, no admin console.

How it works

One PAYONCE-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX key may be activated on up to 3 devices; buy several keys in a single order for a larger team. Each machine activates once (one online check), then works offline. There is no user database tying people to an organisation, and no AI add-on to renew.

What we do not offer

No SSO, no central licence dashboard, no seat reassigning, no usage reporting, and no cloud sync. These are deliberately out of scope (PRD §19). If your procurement requires them, this is not the right tool.

Volume orders, invoices and receipts are handled manually through the storefront (Gumroad / 爱发电) or by email. Contact: hello@payoncepdf.com. Exact volume terms are TODO(team purchasing terms) and will be documented before launch.
